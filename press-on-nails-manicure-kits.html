Frequently asked questions

How long do press-on nails typically last?

Quality gel press-ons applied correctly typically hold for one to two weeks, similar to a budget salon gel manicure — prep, like pushing back cuticles and lightly buffing the natural nail, matters more for wear time than the brand of press-on.

Do press-on nails damage your natural nails?

Removal technique matters more than the product itself — soaking the adhesive loose rather than peeling or prying the press-on off is what actually protects the natural nail underneath.

What's the difference between dip powder and regular polish?

Dip powder builds a harder, more chip-resistant layer without needing a UV lamp, while regular polish is faster to apply and remove — dip typically lasts longer but needs a proper kit and more setup time per application.
