Frequently asked questions

Is UV or LED better for curing gel polish?

LED cures faster and most newer gel formulas are made for it, but UV lamps still work with a wider range of older gel polish brands — a dual UV/LED lamp avoids having to check which one your polish needs.

How long does it take to cure gel polish under a lamp?

Typically 30 to 60 seconds per coat under LED, or around 2 minutes under UV — exact times depend on the lamp's wattage and the specific gel polish brand's instructions, which are worth following over a generic rule of thumb.

Is at-home gel lamp curing safe for skin?

Keeping hands at rest inside the lamp for the recommended time and not exceeding it is the standard approach — some people also apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen on their hands before curing as an extra precaution, which is a personal choice rather than something any specific lamp changes.
