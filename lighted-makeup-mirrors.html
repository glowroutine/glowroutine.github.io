Frequently asked questions

What light setting should I use for everyday makeup?

Natural/daylight setting is the safest general-purpose choice since it's closest to how you'll actually be seen outdoors — save warm and cool settings for checking specific lighting scenarios.

Is a magnifying mirror bad for skin perception?

Strong magnification (10x+) can make normal skin texture look more prominent than it really is — use it for precision tasks, not for general skin assessment.

Battery or plug-in — which lasts longer?

Plug-in mirrors have unlimited runtime but need an outlet nearby; rechargeable battery mirrors are more portable but need periodic charging — pick based on where the mirror will actually live.
