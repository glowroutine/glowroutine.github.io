Which one should you get?

Start with what's actually going wrong in your current routine: if color is uneven, the mitt is the cheapest fix; if you want a gentler ramp-up, the gradual lotion is the safest; if you want a faster, more even result for an occasion, the foam earns its higher price; and if hand application just isn't working for your back and legs, the spray machine solves that specific problem.

Frequently asked questions

How do I avoid orange palms when self-tanning?

Use an application mitt rather than bare hands, and if you do get tanner on your palms, wash quickly with soap before it develops — most "orange hands" complaints trace back to skipping the mitt, not the formula.

How long does a self-tan last?

Most self-tanners fade over 5 to 10 days as skin naturally exfoliates, depending on how often you shower and exfoliate — gradual tanners need reapplication more often but blend fading much less noticeably.

Is a spray tan machine hard to use at home?

There's a learning curve on the first one or two sessions — holding the gun at a consistent distance and overlapping strokes evenly is the main skill, and most machines include a guide for exactly this.
