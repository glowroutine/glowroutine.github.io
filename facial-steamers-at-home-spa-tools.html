Best Facial Steamers & At-Home Spa Tools

Updated August 2026 · Skincare Tech

A facial steamer does one simple thing very well: it opens pores with warm steam so the rest of your skincare routine — cleansing, extractions, masks — actually works better. The differences between models come down to how fine the steam mist is (nano-ionic mists penetrate better than basic steam), how long the tank lasts, and whether you get useful extras like an extraction kit bundled in.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a facial steaming session last?

5-10 minutes is typical — longer isn't necessarily better and can over-dry or irritate skin, especially for sensitive or acne-prone skin.

Can I use tap water in a facial steamer?

Most manufacturers recommend distilled or filtered water to avoid mineral buildup that shortens the device's lifespan — check your specific model's manual.

Should I steam before or after cleansing?

Cleanse first, then steam — steaming on top of makeup or unwashed skin just pushes debris deeper into open pores.
