Best Silk Pillowcases & Overnight Hair Care Essentials

Hair Care

Cotton pillowcases pull moisture out of hair and create friction that leads to frizz, tangles, and breakage overnight — silk and satin don't. This is one of the lowest-effort upgrades in a hair care routine: you change nothing about your routine, you just sleep on a different surface. The options below cover the pillowcase itself plus a couple of adjacent overnight essentials for different hair types.

Frequently asked questions

Does a silk pillowcase really make a difference?

The main, well-supported benefit is reduced friction — less pulling and tangling overnight means less mechanical breakage, particularly noticeable for longer or textured hair.

Silk vs. satin — what's the difference?

Silk is a natural fiber, satin is a weave that can be made from silk or synthetic fibers like polyester — genuine silk breathes better and is typically the pricier, longer-lasting option.

How do I wash a silk pillowcase without damaging it?

Hand-wash in cool water with a gentle detergent, or use a mesh bag on your machine's delicate cycle — avoid the dryer, air dry instead.
