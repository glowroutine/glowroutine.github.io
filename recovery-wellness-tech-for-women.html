Frequently asked questions

Is a percussion massage gun safe to use every day?

For most people, short daily sessions (1-2 minutes per muscle group) are considered safe, but avoid using directly on bones, joints, or injuries, and check with a doctor first if you have a circulatory condition or are pregnant.

How long should a red light therapy session be?

Most body panels recommend 10-20 minutes per session, a few times a week — more isn't necessarily better, and consistency over weeks matters more than any single long session.

Who shouldn't use compression recovery boots?

Anyone with a history of blood clots, deep vein thrombosis, or a serious circulatory condition should check with a doctor before using compression devices — this is one category where medical clearance genuinely matters.
