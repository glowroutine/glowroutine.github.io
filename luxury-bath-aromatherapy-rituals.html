Frequently asked questions

How often should I run an essential oil diffuser?

Most diffusers are safe for a few hours at a time with adequate ventilation — running one continuously in a closed room isn't necessary for the scent benefit and can be overwhelming; intermittent cycles (often built into the device) work well.

Are bath salts actually good for sore muscles?

Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate) dissolved in warm water is commonly used for muscle relaxation, and warm water alone helps circulation — the evidence for skin absorption of magnesium specifically is mixed, but the warm-soak effect itself is well supported.

What should I look for in bathrobe fabric quality?

GSM (grams per square meter) is the actual quality metric to check — higher GSM (400+) means a thicker, more absorbent, longer-lasting robe, regardless of what the listing photos suggest.
