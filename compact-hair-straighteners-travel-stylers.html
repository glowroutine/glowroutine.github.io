Frequently asked questions

Do I need dual voltage if I'm not traveling internationally?

No — if you're only using it domestically, dual voltage doesn't matter; it only becomes relevant when plugging into a different country's electrical standard.

What temperature is safe for fine or damaged hair?

Generally under 300°F (150°C) for fine or damaged hair, with higher settings reserved for thick or coarse hair — most quality irons let you set this precisely rather than only offering high/low.

Ceramic vs. titanium plates — which is better?

Ceramic heats more evenly and is gentler, generally recommended for fine to normal hair; titanium heats faster and holds heat better, often preferred for thick or coarse hair.
