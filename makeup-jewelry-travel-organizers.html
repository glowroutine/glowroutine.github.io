Frequently asked questions

What's the best way to organize makeup brushes specifically?

Upright cylinder holders keep bristles from bending, which flat storage doesn't — most acrylic organizers include at least one upright compartment for this reason.

How do I stop jewelry from tangling while traveling?

Separate compartments with individual hooks or slots (rather than one open pouch) are what actually prevent tangling — a single zip pouch with everything loose is the most common cause of knots.

Is acrylic organizer storage durable long-term?

Good-quality acrylic holds up well to normal use, though it can scratch over years of contact with metal jewelry or hard product packaging — some care in loading it helps it last.
