Which one should you get?

If you only want one thing from this list, pick based on which feature bothers you more day to day: the eyebrow shaper and heated curler solve an immediate styling problem, while the two serums are slower investments in what you're styling. Most people get the best return pairing one quick tool with one serum rather than picking all four at once.

Frequently asked questions

How long do eyebrow and eyelash growth serums take to work?

Most serums ask for 6 to 8 weeks of consistent daily use before a visible difference shows up — stopping and restarting resets that clock, so consistency matters more than the specific brand.

Is a heated eyelash curler safe for everyday use?

Yes, when used as directed — the heat levels are designed to be gentle on lashes, but as with any heat tool near the eyes, test on a small section first if you have particularly sensitive eyes.

Can you use a brow serum and a lash serum at the same time?

Yes, they're typically formulated to be used together since they target different areas — just apply each to its intended area only, since lash serums are formulated for the lash line specifically.
