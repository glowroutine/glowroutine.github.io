About GlowRoutine

Research-backed beauty and self-care picks, without the sponsored-post noise.

Who we are

GlowRoutine is a small, independent site focused on one specific problem: too much beauty advice online is either a sponsored post dressed up as a recommendation, or a "best of" list that was never actually compared against anything. We started this site to do the comparison work properly — skincare tech, hair care, and makeup organization, picked on what the reviews and specs actually say, not on what pays the highest commission.

How we choose what to recommend

We don't test every product physically — what we do instead is research each pick carefully before it goes on the site:

  • We compare products within a category on the things that actually matter — real specs, what a device is genuinely good for versus what the marketing implies, and how it holds up based on buyer reviews.
  • We read through publicly available customer reviews and ratings, looking specifically for patterns — recurring complaints about durability, sizing, or performance are treated as more reliable than a single five-star review.
  • We favor products with a solid volume of reviews and recognizable trust signals over lesser-known items with little feedback, unless a lesser-known item is clearly the better fit for a specific need.
  • We organize each guide by tier (budget, mid-range, premium) so you can compare honestly instead of only seeing the option that pays the highest commission.

How we make money

GlowRoutine is a participant in the Amazon Associates program. When you click one of our product links and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. This is the only way the site makes money; we don't accept payment from brands to feature their products, and commission never determines which product we call the "best pick" in a guide. Full details are on our affiliate disclosure page.

Our promise

Every guide is written to help you make a decision quickly, not to talk you into the most expensive option. If a budget pick is genuinely the smartest choice for most people, we'll say so. If you have feedback on a guide, or a category you'd like us to cover, we'd love to hear about it.
