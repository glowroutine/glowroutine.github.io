Frequently asked questions

Does sunlight really damage perfume?

Yes — UV light and heat both break down fragrance molecules over time, which is why perfume counters use closed cases and why storing bottles away from a sunny windowsill genuinely extends how a fragrance smells over months and years.

How do I clean a refillable atomizer between different fragrances?

Rinse with isopropyl alcohol and let it air dry completely before refilling — leftover residue from a previous fragrance can noticeably alter how the next one smells.

What's the ideal storage temperature for fragrance?

Cool and stable is best — avoid bathrooms (humidity and temperature swings) and direct sunlight; a bedroom dresser or closet is generally a better long-term home for a collection than a bathroom counter.
