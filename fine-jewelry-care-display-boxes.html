Frequently asked questions

Can I clean all my jewelry in an ultrasonic cleaner?

No — solid gold, platinum, and untreated diamonds are generally safe, but pearls, opals, emeralds, and other soft or fracture-filled stones can be damaged by the vibration. When in doubt about a specific piece, skip the ultrasonic cleaner or ask a jeweler.

Why does my silver jewelry tarnish so fast?

Silver reacts with sulfur compounds in the air and on skin (from lotions, perfume, and humidity) — storing pieces in an airtight anti-tarnish bag between wears slows this reaction significantly compared to an open dish or drawer.

Should gold and silver jewelry be stored together?

It's fine to store them near each other, but keep pieces separated (individual compartments or pouches) rather than piled together — metal-on-metal contact is what actually causes scratching, not proximity.
